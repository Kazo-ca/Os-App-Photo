# Third-party notices

Kazo Photo is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). It ships third-party work under its own licences:

- **PhotoCraft** (ArtCraft Team, built from the fork `Kazo-ca/photocraft`), in
  `src/Kazo.Photo/wwwroot/host/`: its licences, `NOTICE` and `ATTRIBUTION.md` sit next to its
  build there (`tools/vendor-photocraft.sh`).
- **Fonts** (SIL Open Font License 1.1), in `src/Kazo.Photo/wwwroot/host/fonts/`: the Latin
  catalog of `storytold/craft-fonts` at the commit of `tools/craft-fonts.rev`, less Inter, which
  PhotoCraft already carries (`tools/vendor-fonts.sh`). Each family's `OFL.txt` (copyright and
  licence) is in its folder; `fonts/manifest.txt` lists every file with its SHA-256 and source.
